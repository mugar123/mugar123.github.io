# Logo credits

All marks are registered trademarks of their respective owners and are used here only to identify the service or institution PaperTok connects to. Nothing implies endorsement. Files were cropped to the symbol, given a square viewBox or canvas, and (for PNGs) a transparent background where possible; no mark was redrawn or recoloured except where noted.
